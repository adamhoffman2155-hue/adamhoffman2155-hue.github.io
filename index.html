Frieren Fern Uploader

Frieren Fern Uploader is a private application operated by Adam Hoffman for managing his own YouTube channel, Frieren's Fern. The application is used by the operator only; it is not offered to the public and has no other users.

The application uses the YouTube Data API v3, authorized through Google OAuth 2.0, to upload custom thumbnail images to videos on the operator's own YouTube channel. That is its only function.
